Replace Wireless Mouse Battery

Restore wireless mouse operation by safely replacing the depleted battery.

Parts: wireless mouse, AA battery, battery cover

1

Turn off the mouse 1–13 s

Flip the mouse over and slide the power switch to OFF.

✓ Check: Switch is in OFF position and sensor light is off.

2

Open battery cover 13–20 s

Hold the mouse steady and slide the rear battery cover off.

✓ Check: Cover loosens and separates from mouse body.

3

Remove old battery 20–30 s

Pull the old battery out of the compartment.

✓ Check: Battery is lifted clear of the compartment.

⚠ Avoid touching leaking or corroded contacts.

4

Inspect polarity markings 30–36 s

Look into the empty compartment to locate the + and - polarity marks.

✓ Check: Compartment is empty, polarity marks visible, cover set aside.

5

Insert new battery 36–44 s

Place the new AA battery to match the + and - polarity marks.

✓ Check: Battery sits flat and secure in the compartment.

⚠ Do not reverse polarity.

6

Close cover and power on 46–55 s

Slide the battery cover back until it clicks, then slide the power switch to ON.

✓ Check: Cover is flush and sensor light glows.
